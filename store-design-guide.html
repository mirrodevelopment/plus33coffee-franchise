SECTION 01 — BRAND DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
French Heritage & Spatial Luxury

Sartorial Space Planning

PLUS33 is designed as a sanctuary. Drawing inspiration from modern Parisian retail boutiques and classical French brasseries, our architecture combines clean geometric lines with highly detailed materials like brushed gold brass, textured warm ivory walls, and natural oak.

Our layout maximizes operational speed while providing an atmosphere of slow luxury. Every physical contact point—from the custom marble countertops to the soft lighting and acoustics—is curated to deliver an unforgettable premium customer journey.

SECTION 12 — EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATION LAYOUT
Bar Machinery Positioning

Espresso Machine & Barista Layout Standards

Bar equipment must be set to allow smooth operations. The espresso machine must sit centered on the front counter in clear view of the customer ordering line.

Grinders are positioned directly adjacent to the machine to allow the barista to work with minimal wrist stress and no body rotation.

SECTION 13 — FURNITURE STANDARDS
Premium Ergonomics

Café Chairs & Seating Dimensions

All seating furniture must use natural solid wood frame structures. Cushion fabrics must be fire-retardant and pass high Martindale rub tests (min 50,000 cycles).

Mandatory Chair Heights:
- Seat Heights: 450 mm from floor level.
- Dining Table Height: 750 mm from floor level.
- Bar Stool Seat Height: 750 mm (Counter at 1050 mm).

SECTION 14 — BRANDING & SIGNAGE
Visual Brand Identity

Signage Scale & Installation Parameters

External logo signage must use 3D PVD brass lettering with halo LED backlighting. The signature logo crest must match the color code #B88A4A.

Digital menu boards must be mounted vertically, framed in matching matte black casings, with cables completely hidden from view.
