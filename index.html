FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with PLUS33 Café Français.

01 |

What is the total investment required?

The total investment varies by store format and location. On average, it ranges between ₹35 Lakhs to ₹75 Lakhs including interiors, equipment, and initial inventory.

02 |

How much space is required for a franchise?

Store formats range from 150 sq.ft. (Kiosk) to 1500+ sq.ft. (Flagship Café) depending on your target location and city tier.

03 |

What kind of support will I receive?

We provide end-to-end support including real estate assistance, store architectural design, staff training, supply chain, and local marketing.

04 |

What are the royalty and other fees?

Our royalty fee is set at a competitive 5% to 6% of gross sales, enabling strong unit profitability and continuous brand innovation.

05 |

How long does it take to open a store?

Typically, a new store takes between 45 to 60 days from signing the agreement to complete fit-out and grand opening.

06 |

Can I choose my store location?

Yes! You can suggest prime locations. Our real estate team will conduct feasibility and catchment analysis before final approval.

07 |

What is the expected ROI?

Most stores achieve 20% to 30% EBITDA margins, delivering complete capital payback within an estimated 24 to 36 months.

08 |

Do you provide training?

Yes. We conduct intensive 3-week operational and barista training programs for your entire store team prior to opening.

09 |

Is previous experience in F&B required?

No prior F&B experience is necessary. Our standardized operating manuals, tech stack, and field managers simplify operations.

10 |

How do I get started?

Simply click 'Book a Consultation' below or submit an application form. Our franchise team will get in touch within 24 hours.
